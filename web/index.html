Frequently Asked Questions

How does TunnelSats keep my home IP hidden?

TunnelSats runs directly inside your target Lightning node container (LND or Core Lightning) via native in-container WireGuard:

  • In-Container Tunnel (wg0): WireGuard operates inside your node's own container. Incoming peer packets arrive directly at <tunnel-ip>:9735 where the daemon is already listening—eliminating host-level port forwarding and multi-node port conflicts.
  • Egress Routing: Container-level policy routing (table 51820) directs all clearnet traffic (handshakes, channel gossip, ping/pong acknowledgments) through the encrypted tunnel while it is up.
  • Kill Switch Caveat: The tunnel and its routing belong to your Lightning node package. With current node builds, clearnet traffic can fall back to your home connection if wg0 goes down; a fix in the node packages is pending.
  • Tor Hybrid Coexistence: Tor traffic continues uninterrupted across the container bridge network (eth0), maintaining seamless hybrid routing.

Zero System Gateways: You never need to configure anything under StartOS System → Gateways, toggle interface firewalls, or manage outbound gateway rules.

How do I order a new subscription directly on StartOS?

Our native in-container storefront makes ordering instant, private, and frictionless:

  1. Select your target Lightning node (LND or CLN), server region, and plan duration.
  2. Your device generates sovereign cryptographic WireGuard keys locally in-process with zero KYC and zero server key custody.
  3. Pay the Lightning invoice via QR code or WebLN / "Open in Wallet".
  4. Once paid, TunnelSats automatically passes the configuration to your selected Lightning node. Accept the 1-click prompt on your node: "Route [Node] through the TunnelSats tunnel". Your node brings up its internal WireGuard interface, announces its external address, and routes peer traffic securely.
How do renewals work? What happens if my subscription expires?

You can click Renew Subscription on this dashboard at any time before or after expiration:

  • Zero Reconfiguration: Renewals seamlessly extend your existing cryptographic keys, assigned public IP, and port bindings. You never need to reconfigure your node, change network settings, or restart your Lightning daemon.
  • If Expired: Inbound routing pauses safely until renewed. Your keys are preserved for convenient reactivation.
Can I set up automatic, hands-free subscription renewals?

Yes, native on-box Nostr Wallet Connect (NWC) auto-renewals are supported:

  • Sovereign On-Box Automation: You can connect a Nostr Wallet Connect (NIP-47) pairing string from your local Alby Hub, LNbits, or mobile wallet directly within StartOS. Zero accounts or signups on third-party websites required.
  • Capped Allowance & Budget: Your wallet hub enforces a strict monthly sat budget (e.g. 20,000 sats). TunnelSats never holds your node keys, macaroons, or passwords.
  • Automatic Renewal Flow: When your subscription reaches 7 days before expiration, the on-box companion requests a renewal invoice and pays it unprompted via your NWC connection.
  • Fail-Safe Prompt Fallback: If your NWC wallet is disconnected, out of budget, or unconfigured, TunnelSats raises a 1-click Pay Invoice task on your Lightning node so you can manually approve the renewal with one click before the tunnel expires.
How does the 100 GB monthly bandwidth allowance work?

Each subscription includes 100 GB of monthly bandwidth for WireGuard peer transit (channel gossip, routing packets, and keepalive pings):

  • Automatic Calendar Reset: Bandwidth usage resets to 0 GB at 00:00 UTC on the 1st of every calendar month.
  • Zero Surprise Overages: Most routing nodes consume 5–25 GB per month. If 100 GB is reached, the tunnel safely pauses until month-end—no surprise charges or fees.
How is IPv6 handled?

Your Lightning node package decides how IPv6 is routed. Current builds send IPv6 through the tunnel when the configuration's AllowedIPs include ::/0 (TunnelSats configurations do), and block it otherwise.

The Allow IPv6 Endpoint setting only lets TunnelSats hand your node an IPv6 server endpoint to announce.

How do I backup my configuration and audit egress privacy via CLI?

Config Export: Use Services → TunnelSats → Actions → Export WireGuard Configuration in StartOS to securely copy your full configuration.

CLI Route Audit: Verify that your node's outbound IPv4 traffic is fully encapsulated through TunnelSats from your StartOS terminal: 

start-cli package attach lnd -- curl -s https://api.ipify.org

Expected output: your assigned TunnelSats server IP, not your home IP. This checks the tunnel while it is up; it does not test what happens when it goes down.
